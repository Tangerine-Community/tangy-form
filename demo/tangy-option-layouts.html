Option layouts and option images

layout="wrap|squares|fit" works on tangy-radio-buttons, tangy-radio-blocks and tangy-checkboxes. Leave it off for today's look. <option image="..."> shows a picture on radio blocks and checkboxes (radio buttons take none); its size is --tangy-option-image-width / --tangy-option-image-height (default 50px). image-display="top|fill" on radio blocks and checkboxes puts the picture above the text, or makes the picture fill the whole tile (sections 9-12). Leave it off for the image beside the text. The dashed boxes are 360px wide, a phone screen.

1 · Attendance status: radio blocks, fit, with images

Equal boxes on one row. One shared font size shrinks until the longest label fits.

2 · Attendance status: radio buttons, fit, text only

In a layout the radio circle is hidden; a green border shows the choice. Tap anywhere in a box.
